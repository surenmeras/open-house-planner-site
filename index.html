Using Open House Planner

Enter a valid U.S. property address and list price, select the desired date and time, and use Calculate Traffic Probability to estimate traffic potential.

The Find My Best Open House Time feature can compare upcoming time windows to help identify promising opportunities for your next open house.

Forecast Disclaimer

Open House Planner provides estimates and planning assistance. Forecasts, attendance estimates, traffic probabilities, market information, and recommendations are not guarantees of actual attendance, property performance, market conditions, or future results.
