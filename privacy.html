Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Open House Intel LLC ("Open House Intel," "we," "us," or "our") handles information when you use the Open House Intel mobile application and its related services.

We are committed to respecting user privacy, protecting information, and limiting data use to what is reasonably necessary to provide, maintain, and secure the application's functionality.

Information Used by the App

Open House Intel allows users to enter or search for U.S. property addresses and provide property and open house planning information, such as list price, date, and time. This information is used to provide address suggestions, traffic forecasts, timing recommendations, and related app functionality.

Property address searches and related requests may be transmitted to our server and third-party service providers as necessary to process the request and provide app functionality.

Search History

Search information may be processed as part of providing address search and forecasting functionality. Search data is used for app functionality and is not used by Open House Intel for advertising or cross-app tracking.

Open House Intel does not require users to create an account. The app uses an pseudonymous RevenueCat-generated identifier to verify subscription access, enforce monthly forecast limits, and maintain limited operational usage counts. This identifier is not intended to identify you by name and is not used by Open House Intel for advertising or cross-app tracking.

Third-Party Services

Open House Intel uses third-party service providers to support application functionality, subscription management, hosting, and the retrieval of information used to generate open house forecasts and recommendations.

These service providers include:

  • Google Maps Platform - address autocomplete and lookup.
  • U.S. Census Bureau - property address geocoding.
  • OpenStreetMap (Nominatim) - alternative address geocoding.
  • Zippopotam.us - approximate ZIP code locations.
  • National Weather Service - weather forecasts.
  • Ticketmaster - nearby event information.
  • RentCast - real estate market and listing information.
  • RevenueCat - subscription management and verification.
  • Apple - App Store purchases and subscription processing.
  • Render - application hosting and database infrastructure.

Information necessary to provide these services may be transmitted to the relevant service providers. Depending on the requested functionality, this may include property addresses, geographic coordinates, property pricing, event dates and times, subscription identifiers, and technical request information.

Information processed by third-party service providers is subject to their applicable terms, privacy policies, and data handling practices.

Google Maps Platform Services

Open House Intel uses Google Maps Platform, including the Google Places API, to provide property address autocomplete and address lookup functionality.

When users enter or search for a property address, relevant search information may be transmitted to Google to retrieve address suggestions and associated address details.

Google-generated address suggestions are processed to facilitate user-directed property searches and open house planning. Open House Intel does not intentionally maintain a permanent database of Google Places responses or use such responses to train artificial intelligence or machine learning models.

The use of Google Maps Platform services is subject to applicable Google terms and privacy practices:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Maps Additional Terms of Service

Open House Intel generates traffic forecasts using its proprietary forecasting methodology, property information, and independent weather, event, and real estate market data sources.

Advertising and Tracking

Open House Intel does not use search information for third-party advertising and does not use it to track users across apps or websites owned by other companies.

Data Retention

Open House Intel does not intentionally maintain permanent individual user profiles containing property search history. Property addresses and Google-generated address suggestions are processed to provide user-requested application functionality.

Open House Intel retains limited operational records, including pseudonymous subscription identifiers, monthly forecast usage counts, API usage counts, and associated timestamps. These records support subscription verification, usage limits, service administration, and operational security.

Google Maps Platform information is handled subject to applicable Google data usage and retention requirements. Google Places responses are not intentionally stored in a permanent application database.

Limited technical and diagnostic information may be retained by Open House Intel, its hosting provider, or third-party service providers for operational, troubleshooting, and security purposes, subject to applicable requirements.

Data Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information transmitted through Open House Intel. However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Children's Privacy

Open House Intel is intended for real estate professionals and is not designed for children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
